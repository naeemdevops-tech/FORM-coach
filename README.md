# FORM Coach

**Train Smarter. Track Better.**

FORM Coach is an offline-first personal training management application designed to help fitness coaches manage clients, organize workouts, track progress, and streamline their daily coaching activities.

Built for personal trainers who work with **in-person, online, and hybrid clients**, FORM Coach brings essential coaching tools into one simple mobile application.

## Key Features

### Client Management
- Create and manage individual client profiles.
- Organize fitness goals and client information.
- Keep training history and coaching notes.

### Workout Management
- Create personalized workout routines.
- Organize exercises and training sessions.
- Track sets, repetitions, and weights.
- Monitor training performance over time.

### Progress Tracking
- Record body measurements.
- Monitor strength and fitness improvements.
- Organize progress photos.
- Review client development.

### Nutrition Management
- Organize client nutrition plans.
- Track calorie and macronutrient information.
- Maintain nutrition records.

### Offline Functionality
- Designed to work without an internet connection.
- Local data storage using SQLite.
- No cloud account required.
- Trainer-focused application without client login.

*Note: Features listed above describe the intended functionality of FORM Coach and should be verified against the current build.*

## Data Privacy

FORM Coach is designed to store client information locally on the trainer's device rather than on a remote server.

Users should protect their devices and maintain secure backups of important training records.

Uninstalling the application may permanently delete locally stored data.
